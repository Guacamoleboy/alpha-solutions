## User Story 12 - View Operating Hours

As a Pickelball member, I want to view the facility's operating hours, so that I know when I can visit and book a court.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] The member can open the Operating Hours page.
- [ ] The page shows the facility's operating hours for each day of the week.
- [ ] The page clearly shows the opening and closing time for each open day.
- [ ] The page clearly indicates when the facility is closed.
- [ ] The displayed hours match the operating hours configured by the owner.
- [ ] Changes made by the owner to the operating hours are reflected on the member's page.
- [ ] The member can view the operating hours but cannot change them from this page.

## Sub-issues

- [FRONTEND] Provide an Operating Hours page that members can open.
- [BACKEND] Provide the operating hours configured by the owner.
- [FRONTEND] Show the opening and closing time for each open day.
- [FRONTEND] Clearly show which days the facility is closed.
- [FRONTEND] Keep the displayed hours synchronized with the owner's configured operating hours.
- [FRONTEND] Keep the member view read-only.
