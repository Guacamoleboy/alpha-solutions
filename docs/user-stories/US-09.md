## User Story 09 - View Court Booking History

As a Pickelball member, I want to view my previous court bookings and my latest booking date on the member dashboard, so that I can keep track of when and where I have played.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] The member can view a history of their previous court bookings.
- [ ] Each previous booking shows the court, date and booked time period.
- [ ] Previous bookings are shown with the most recent booking first.
- [ ] The member intro dashboard shows the date of the member's most recent completed court booking.
- [ ] The latest booking date on the member dashboard matches the most recent booking in the member's booking history.
- [ ] If the member has no previous court bookings, the member dashboard and booking history show a clear message instead of a date.
- [ ] The booking history is read-only and does not provide functionality to update, cancel or delete previous bookings.

## Sub-issues

- Provide the member's previous court bookings.
- Show the court, date and booked time period for each previous booking.
- Sort the booking history with the most recent booking first.
- Show the date of the most recent completed court booking on the member dashboard.
- Show a clear empty-state message when the member has no previous court bookings.
- Keep the booking history read-only without update, cancel or delete functionality.
